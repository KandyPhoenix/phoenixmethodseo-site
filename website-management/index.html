The four steps after you pay

Four steps, sized for the Spark plan.

  1. Week 1

    We audit the whole site and record where you rank today Us

    We go through every page and list every broken piece. We write down where you rank today, so later changes have something to be measured against. Then everything we found is sorted into one list, most important first.

    • Broken links
    • Slow pages
    • Missing tags
    • Indexing problems
    • Starting rankings

    Your job: nothing.

  2. Weeks 2 to 4

    Fixes go in, most important first Us

    "Fixes" means the problems the audit found and updates to the pages you already have. They come out of your 2 plan hours. The site gets a technical audit every week, and your keywords are checked with it.

    • 2 hours of site fixes and page updates
    • Weekly technical audit
    • Keywords checked every audit

    Your job: nothing. Have a request? Send an email.

  3. Every month

    Audit, fix, update, track Us

    The cycle repeats. Rankings are tracked every month and checked during every weekly audit, so every move is on record.

    Your job: run your business.

  4. Every quarter

    Your report Us

    Every three months you get a report: what we fixed, what moved and what is next.

    Your job: read it.
