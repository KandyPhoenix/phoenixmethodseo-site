SEO and Website Management Questions, Answered

What do you do?

We manage websites and do SEO for small businesses, practices and personal sites. We check the site every week, fix what breaks, make your updates and track where you rank in Google Search Console.

What does it cost?

Monthly plans are $500, $900 and $1,500, sized by page count. One-time setups are $800. One-off services start at $50. See pricing.

Is there a contract?

No. Every plan is month to month. Cancel or change plans with one email. It takes effect at the next billing cycle.

How long until I see results?

We don't give a date. Google can register a fix on your site within hours. Rankings are slower to rise, and movement usually starts about three months in. You see the work and the numbers in every report.

Do you guarantee rankings?

No. No one can guarantee a #1 ranking on Google, so we don't promise one.

What is in the free audit?

It grades three things: technical SEO, on-page SEO and mobile speed. You get a letter grade for each and a plain list of what is broken and why it costs you. No obligation.

Do I need a setup before a monthly plan?

No, but it is faster if your site has never had SEO. Without it, we do the groundwork a little each month with your plan hours.

What if I need more hours than my plan has?

Extra time is $75 an hour for plan clients, with no minimum. The regular rate is $100 an hour, with a half-hour minimum. We confirm the cost before any work is billed. Unused hours don't carry over.

Can I buy one thing without a plan?

Yes. See the order page or Website Updates.

What is not included in a plan?

New blog posts, new pages, Google Business Profile work and social posting are add-ons. Plan clients get 25% off them. On Blaze and Inferno, spare hours can go toward new content when you ask.

How do I pay?

Online by card, debit card or ACH.

Do you work with my kind of business?

Probably. See our Industries page. If you are not sure, ask us.

Is there a referral reward?

Yes. A client who refers a business that buys a plan gets 50% off the next month. Two in the same month and the next month is free.

Where are you?

The Woodlands, Texas. We work with businesses across the country.
