Why Dental Is Different

Local, Urgent, Insurance-Driven, and Treatment-Specific

A dental practice serves the area around it, so we treat local search as the base of the work. Local results are mainly based on relevance, distance and popularity, and a business with complete and accurate information is more likely to show. An emergency dental search is a different need from a routine one, so emergency care gets its own page with your hours, your phone number and what to do next. Cosmetic and specialty searches (Invisalign, veneers, implants) name a specific treatment, so each treatment gets its own page instead of one line on a services list.

Frequently Asked

Dental SEO Questions

How is dental SEO different from general SEO?

Dental SEO is local SEO first. The searches we build for are local and often urgent: "dentist near me," "emergency dental," "pediatric dentist [city]." Insurance, financing, and specialty positioning all shape the content plan, so we build a page for each of them instead of one general services page.

How long until I see new patients from SEO?

Dental SEO timelines vary by local competition and where your site starts. Some changes show in hours and others take months. We start with the local work: Google Business Profile setup. Then we build the pages for searches like "general dentist [city]" or "emergency dentist," and then the specialty terms (Invisalign, cosmetic dentistry). We do not promise a date or a number of new patients.

Should I specialize my site or market everything equally?

We recommend dedicated pages. A general dentist with a well-optimized page each for cosmetic work, Invisalign, emergency services, and pediatric dentistry gives every one of those searches a specific answer. A homepage that lists everything with equal emphasis does not. A dedicated page gives each service its own title, heading and content to match the search.

Do I need separate content for each insurance I accept?

Yes, or at least a dedicated insurance/financing page. It gives searches like "dentist that takes [insurance]" and "dentist payment plans" a page to land on, and it answers the question before a patient has to call.

What about patient reviews? Do those help rankings?

Yes. More reviews and positive ratings can help your local ranking. With monthly Google Business Profile management, we check for new reviews and tell you when one needs your reply.

Can you handle multi-location practices?

Yes. For a multi-location practice we build a page for each location, set up a Google Business Profile for each location and write its posts, and keep the location pages distinct so they do not duplicate each other.
