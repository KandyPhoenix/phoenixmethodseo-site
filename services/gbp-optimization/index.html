Inside the Profile

Every Part of Your Google Business Profile, Covered

Seven parts of the profile. Here is what we cover in each one.

Categories and Attributes

We check your primary category, secondary categories, service attributes and accessibility markers, and correct any that are wrong. You get a profile filed under what you actually do.

Business Description

We rewrite the description to say what you do, who you serve and what makes you different, in plain words with no keyword stuffing. You get a description that reads like your business, not a list of search terms.

Services and Products

We list every service you offer with a clean name, a short description and a price range where it fits. A customer reading your profile can see what you sell before they call.

Photos

We upload the photos you send us of your location, your team and your work, each with a proper file name. Your own photos, not stock images.

Questions and Answers

Where your profile has a questions section, we answer the common customer questions there in your voice, so the answers come from you.

Posts and Updates

A post every week: offers, updates and events, each written and designed with an image.

Reviews

We check for new reviews and tell you when one needs a reply. The reply comes from you, the owner, so it is in your voice.

Problems We Fix

Google Business Profile Problems We Fix

These are the problems we look for on a profile, and what we do about each one.

  • Wrong primary category. For example, a cosmetic dentistry practice listed only as a general dentist, or a licensed therapist listed as a counselor. We correct it.
  • Name, address and phone that do not match. The profile says one thing and your site or a directory listing says another. We find the mismatches and correct them.
  • No posts, no photos, no activity. We post every week and upload the photos you send, so the profile shows a business that is open and working.
  • Empty questions section. Where the profile has one, we add the common customer questions and answer them in your voice.

How Google Chooses Local Results

Local results are mainly based on relevance, distance and popularity. We cannot change how far a searcher is from you, so the profile work is aimed at the other two. We fill in every part of the profile and correct what is wrong, and we tell you when a review needs a reply.

There is no way to pay for a better local ranking. What you pay us for is the work on the profile.

Frequently Asked

Google Business Profile Questions

Is Google Business Profile the same as Google My Business?

Yes. Google renamed Google My Business to Google Business Profile. It is the same product with a different name. Older articles that say Google My Business or GMB mean the same thing.

How often should I post on my Google Business Profile?

We post every week, 4 posts a month: offers, updates or events. Posts older than 6 months are archived unless a date range is set.

Can you handle negative reviews?

We check for new reviews and tell you when one needs a reply. The reply comes from you, the owner. We do not buy reviews.

Is this the same as local SEO?

No. Profile management keeps your Google listing current every month. The Local SEO Setup is a one-time $800 service that also covers your directory listings and the local schema on your website.

What if I don't have a Google Business Profile yet?
