Local SEO vs National SEO

Which Do You Actually Need?

Local SEO wins when...

Your customers search "[service] near me", "[service] in [city]", or are actively looking for businesses within driving distance. You have a physical location or a service area. You compete against other businesses nearby, not across the country.

National SEO wins when...

You sell online, ship products, or deliver services remotely. Your customers can be anywhere, so where you are based does not decide the sale. The work is on content depth and technical performance rather than proximity and citations.

Some businesses need both: a local layer for nearby buyers and a national, technical layer for everyone else. We'll tell you honestly which applies to you.

Frequently Asked

Local SEO Questions We Get Often

How long does local SEO take to work?

We don't give you a date. Local SEO timelines vary by market competition and where you start. Some changes show in hours and others take months. We start with the fixes that are quickest to make.

What is NAP consistency and why does it matter so much?

NAP stands for Name, Address, and Phone. Your business is listed on directories, aggregators, and social platforms, and the details do not always match: "St" in one place and "Street" in another, different suite formats, an old phone number left on a stale directory. A business with complete and accurate information is more likely to show up in local search results. We make your details match, on your profile and everywhere else they appear.

Is Google Business Profile optimization enough on its own?

We don't treat it as enough on its own. The profile is the first thing we set up. We pair it with consistent NAP across your listings, local schema markup on your site, and locally relevant content.

Do I need local SEO if I serve clients nationally?

Only if you have physical locations or service areas where walk-in or regional clients matter. A purely national e-commerce or remote software business needs different work, which we cover under Technical SEO and Content SEO. If you have a brick-and-mortar location, we recommend local SEO.
