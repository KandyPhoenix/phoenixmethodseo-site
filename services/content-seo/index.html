How We Write

The Editorial Standard

Every piece we produce meets these non-negotiables:

  • Subject matter accuracy: fact-checked against authoritative sources; no hallucinated statistics or fake citations
  • Search intent match: written to serve the actual query (informational, commercial, navigational)
  • Scannable structure: clear H-hierarchy, short paragraphs, bulleted lists, highlighted takeaways
  • Internal linking: every new piece connects to at least 2-3 existing related pages

Frequently Asked

Content SEO Questions

How many blog posts should I publish per month?

We recommend quality over frequency: 2 to 4 substantive, well-researched posts a month rather than many thin ones. We'd rather write one piece that answers a question completely than publish filler.

What's a topic cluster and why does it matter?

A topic cluster is a pillar page plus a group of supporting pages on related subtopics, all internally linked. It matters because everything you have on a subject can be found from one place. We plan content in clusters so a subject is covered completely, not with one thin post.

Can I just use AI to write SEO content?

AI-generated content that gets edited by a human who knows the subject can work. We don't recommend publishing raw AI output without an editor who knows the subject, because errors get through. Every piece we send out is checked against official sources, reviewed by a person, and sent to you to confirm the facts about your business.

How long does content SEO take to work?

We don't give you a date. Content SEO timelines vary by topic competition, search intent, and where your site starts. Some changes show in hours and others take months. Plan for content as long-term work.

Do you do content for YMYL topics (health, finance, legal)?

Yes. We write healthcare content. On YMYL topics we do extra E-E-A-T work: author credentials, demonstrable expertise, and authoritative citations. See our Healthcare SEO page for how we handle this.

Can you refresh old posts instead of writing new ones?

Yes. A refresh means adding depth, updating data, improving internal links, and refining on-page elements on a post you already have. We audit what you have first and recommend the mix of refreshes and new posts.
