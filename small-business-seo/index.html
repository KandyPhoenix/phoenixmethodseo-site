The Six Areas of Small Business SEO

Small business SEO with us covers six areas. Here is what each one is and what you get.

Google Business Profile

Your Google Business Profile is the listing that shows your business on Google Search and Maps. If you have a location or a service area, we start here. We fill the profile in, correct what is wrong and keep it active with posts. Google says a business with complete and accurate information is more likely to show up in local search.

Frequently asked

Small Business SEO Questions

How long does small business SEO take to show results?

We don't give you a date, and we won't guess before we have audited your site. Google says some changes show in hours and others take months. What you get from us is the work, on record, and the numbers that go with it.

How much should a small business spend on SEO?

We can only tell you what we charge. Monthly plans are $500, $900 and $1,500, sized by page count. One-time setups are $800. One-off services start at $20. Every price is on the pricing page.

Can small business SEO really compete with bigger competitors?

We can't promise you will outrank anyone. Google says "No one can guarantee a #1 ranking on Google." What we do is focus on local searches and on specific searches for what you sell, not on broad national terms. Google says local results are mainly based on relevance, distance and popularity, and that there is no way to pay for a better local ranking.

Do you require long-term contracts?

No. Every monthly plan is month to month. Cancel or change plans with one email, and it takes effect at the next billing cycle. If we are not earning our keep, you should not be stuck.
