Website Update Services, No Monthly Plan Required

What it is

You send us the change. We make it. You pay for the time it took.

Who it's for

Owners with a site that mostly works and a list of things that are wrong on it. Old hours. Old prices. A staff page with someone who left. A typo that has been there for a year.

What's included

  • Text, price and hours changes
  • Photo swaps
  • Rewrites of existing pages
  • Fixes to things that broke

A brand-new page is $250. A blog post is $150 to $200.

Price

$100 an hour, with a half-hour minimum of $50. We tell you the time and cost first. No payment until you approve. On a monthly plan the rate is $75 an hour, with no minimum.

What happens after you ask

You send the request. We reply with the scope and the cost. You approve. We do the work and tell you when it is live.

What we work on

We work on most website platforms, including WordPress, Squarespace and custom-coded sites. For Shopify, Webflow and Wix sites, we send you a quote before we begin. If your site is on a different platform, email us at hello@phoenixmethodseo.com and we will confirm that we can work on it before any work begins.
