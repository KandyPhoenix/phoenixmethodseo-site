What drives the price

Two things. How many pages your site has. How many hours of fixes and updates you want each month. That is the whole formula.

The terms

  • Month to month. Cancel or change plans with one email. It takes effect at the next billing cycle.
  • Plan clients get 25% off one-off services and pay $75 an hour, instead of $100, for work beyond the hours their plan includes, with no minimum.
  • We confirm the cost of extra work before it is billed.
  • Unused hours do not carry over.
  • Pay online by card, debit card or ACH.

What we won't do to you

  • No long-term contracts. Every plan is month to month. Cancel any time.
  • No setup fees beyond what is listed. $800 covers a site of up to 10 pages, and extra pages are $50 each.
  • One point of contact. You deal with the same contact every time.
  • No black-hat tactics. No link schemes, no hidden text, no mass-produced content. We work inside Google's published guidelines.
  • No vanity reports. You see what was done, why, and what moved. Numbers you can act on.
  • No upsell pressure. We tell you plainly when you don't need more, and when you do.

Pricing questions

How much should a small business spend on SEO?

We can only tell you what we charge. Monthly plans are $500, $900 and $1,500, sized by page count. A one-time setup is $800 for a site of up to 10 pages, and extra pages are $50 each. One-off services start at $50.

Is there a minimum contract length?

No. Every Phoenix Method plan is month to month. You can cancel any time. We would rather keep earning your business every month than lock you into something you regret.

What's included vs what costs extra?

A monthly plan includes everything listed for that plan: the weekly technical audit, monthly Google Search Console rank tracking, the quarterly report, and your hours for fixes and updates. New content, Google Business Profile work and social posting are add-ons. On Blaze and Inferno, spare hours can go toward new content if you ask. Anything larger, such as a major redesign, is quoted separately. Plan clients get 25% off one-off services and pay $75 an hour, in place of $100, for work beyond the hours their plan includes.

Can I cancel and come back later?

Yes. Every plan is month to month, with no contract and no penalty. If you need to stop, stop. If you want to come back, we pick up where you left off.

Do you offer discounts for annual payment?

No. Plans are month to month, and we price them that way.

Do you have a referral program?

Yes. Current clients who refer a business that buys a package get 50% off their next month. Refer two businesses that buy in the same month and your next month is free. Have them mention your name when they reach out.

What payment methods do you accept?

Pay online by credit card, debit card or ACH. Invoices are generated each month. We do not take crypto or wire transfers.
